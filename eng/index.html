ABOUT US

Since 2019, we have been creating 2D animated films and series. Over this time, we have completed numerous projects and learned even more along the way. However, we have no intention of stopping here; every new production is an opportunity for us to hone our skills, experiment with new ideas, and continue improving at what we do.

Initially, the team began as an engaging hobby—a way to escape into a world of our own creation, where everyone involved could have fun and pursue their personal interests. Today, we strive for the highest possible quality—in terms of both audiovisual execution and storytelling—ensuring our narratives are immersive, original, and packed with emotion and plot twists. Above all, we want them to be truly enjoyable to watch.

In our animation work, we employ a variety of techniques, styles, and unconventional approaches to achieve our desired vision. We frequently incorporate 3D elements and scenes into our productions—sometimes to streamline and accelerate the creative process, and other times to achieve effects that would be difficult to realize using traditional 2D methods alone.

You can find our animations, illustrations, and other graphic work across a wide range of platforms, from social media to streaming services.
