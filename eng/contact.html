FAQ

Do you undertake long-term cooperation?
Of course!
What is the cost of making an animation?
The cost of making an animation is not imposed in advance and depends, among others, on: on the length of the film, animation style and additional services such as editing. We evaluate each proposal individually and provide the cost only after carefully reviewing the offer.
How long does it take to make an animation?
The time needed to make an animated film depends, just like its cost, mainly on the length of the film and the style in which the film will be created.
What services do you offer beyond just animation?
In addition to animating, we also offer film directing, script writing, editing, sound design, character design and much more!
